01 / THE PERSON BEHIND THE RESEARCH

About me.

At the intersection of language, disciplinary knowledge, and academic communication.

I am a Professor of Applied Linguistics at Guangzhou Maritime University, China, where I also serve as Vice Dean of the School of International Exchange.

My research interests include applied linguistics, academic discourse, English for Specific Purposes, genre studies, and corpus linguistics..

I obtained my PhD in Linguistics and Applied Linguistics from Beijing Foreign Studies University in 2021, where I conducted my doctoral research under the supervision of Professor Jiajin Xu. My recent publications have appeared in Applied Linguistics (Oxford University Press), English for Specific Purposes (Elsevier), International Journal of Applied Linguistics (Wiley), and IRAL – International Review of Applied Linguistics in Language Teaching (De Gruyter).
